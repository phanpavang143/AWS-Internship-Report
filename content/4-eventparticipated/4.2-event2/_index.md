# Event Summary: “FCAJ Community Day”

### Event Overview
*   **Event Title**: Mini Meetup – First Cloud AI Journey

*   **Date**: 09:00 – 12:00 | Saturday, August 15, 2026
*   **Location**:AWS Office – Grand Terra Tower, 36 Cat Linh, Dong Da, Hanoi
*   **Organizer**: FCAJ Community
*   **Role**: Participant

---

### Event Objectives
* Learn how to leverage LinkedIn and build a personal brand to unlock new opportunities in your education and career.

* Gain foundational knowledge and insights for those looking to enter the world of Cloud computing and build a solid base for the journey ahead.

* Explore common hosting and server models, understand the differences between them, and—most importantly—determine the right solution for specific needs.

---

### Key Highlights
* **LinkedIn & Personal Branding:**
  * Build a professional LinkedIn profile.
  * Expand your network and career opportunities.

* **Foundational Cloud Knowledge:**
  * Familiarize yourself with basic Cloud concepts.
  * Define a learning and development roadmap in the Cloud field.

* **Hosting & Servers:**
  * Explore common hosting and server models.
  * Learn how to select the right solution for specific projects.

---

### Key Learnings
* **Mindset and Approach:**
  * Proactively build a personal brand and define a career path.
  * Adopt a mindset of selecting technologies suited to specific practical needs.

* **Technical Knowledge:**
  * Grasp fundamental concepts of Cloud technology.
  * Understand common hosting and server models.

* **Practical Experience:**
  * Learn how to use LinkedIn for networking.
  * Gain insights into selecting and deploying hosting models.

* **Practical Application:**
  * Create a professional LinkedIn profile.
  * Select appropriate server/hosting solutions for projects.

* **Event Experience:**
  * Gain practical perspectives from the speaker.
  * Have opportunities to learn and exchange ideas regarding Cloud and technology.

* **Key Takeaways:**
  * Proactive learning and personal branding are essential.
  * Technology selection should be based on project requirements and goals.
