Having completed my meaningful internship journey in the **First Cloud AI Journey** program, I would like to share my personal feedback and suggestions to help improve the experience for future cohorts:

### General Evaluation

**1. Learning and Working Environment:**
*   An environment that fosters proactive, practical, and professional learning.
*   Opportunities for hands-on practice using the AWS platform.
*   Encouragement of self-study and proactive problem-solving.

**2. Support from Mentors & the Admin Team:**
*   Mentors provide guidance and answer questions throughout the learning process.
*   Administrators offer timely support regarding accounts, materials, and practical exercises.
*   Receipt of constructive feedback and direction to improve performance.

**3. Relevance to Computer Science Major:**
*   Reinforcement of knowledge regarding cloud computing systems and technologies.
*   Application of programming and system knowledge to eal-world labs.
*   Development of skills in application deployment and operations.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities:**
*   Exposure to and hands-on experience with various AWS services.
*   Development of skills in Cloud, Docker, CI/CD, and troubleshooting.
*   Cultivation of self-learning abilities and research skills for new technologies.

**5. Culture & Team Spirit:**
*   An open, friendly, and supportive environment.
*   Encouragement of knowledge exchange and experience sharing.
*   Fostering a spirit of collaboration and teamwork.

---

### Survey Questions

* **What did you enjoy most about the internship?**

  Getting hands-on experience with AWS and applying knowledge through practical labs, while also receiving guidance and support from my mentor.

* **What improvements would you suggest for future cohorts?**

  Incorporating more real-world projects and increasing the amount of hands-on practice time would give participants more opportunities to apply what they have learned.

* **Would you recommend this program to friends?**

  Yes. The program provides practical knowledge of AWS and cloud technology, making it a great fit for students interested in exploring and developing skills in the field of cloud computing.

---

### Suggestions & Expectations


*   **Recommendations**: Increase the number of lab exercises and real-world projects—particularly in Cloud, DevOps, Docker, and CI/CD—to provide students with opportunities to apply their specialized knowledge.

*   **Expectations**: Provide more in-depth guidance sessions and opportunities to work on real-world projects to help students enhance their professional skills and better prepare for their careers after graduation.
